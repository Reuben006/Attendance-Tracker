Frequently Asked Questions

Home

What is on the Home page?

The Home page is a full Dashboard. It shows your user profile, overall attendance percentage, a weekly summary, today's scheduled lectures, quick action buttons for all features, and any attendance alerts all in one place.

How do I set up my profile?

Click the "Edit" button next to your profile on the Dashboard. A form will appear where you can enter your Name, Roll Number, Email, and set your Attendance Threshold (%). Hit "Save" to update your details.

What does "Overall Attendance" show?

The Overall Attendance card displays your combined attendance percentage across all subjects. It starts at 0% and updates automatically as you mark attendance.

What is the Weekly Summary?

The Weekly Summary gives you a quick overview of how many classes you attended versus how many were held during the current week, helping you stay on track day by day.

What does the "Today" section show?

The Today section displays the lectures scheduled for the current day based on your timetable. If no timetable is set up yet, it will show "No lectures".

What are Quick Actions?

Quick Actions are shortcut buttons on the Dashboard that take you directly to Mark Attendance, Edit Attendance, Set Timetable, Stats, Bunk Calculator, and Settings so you never have to dig through the navbar.

What are Alerts?

Alerts notify you if any subject's attendance drops below your set threshold. They load automatically on the Dashboard so you are always aware of subjects that need immediate attention.

What is the Attendance Threshold in my profile?

The Attendance Threshold is the minimum percentage you want to maintain across subjects. It is set in your profile and is used by the Dashboard alerts to warn you when you're at risk.

Attendance

How do I mark today's attendance?

Go to the Attendance page. You'll see today's subjects listed based on your timetable. For each subject, mark it as Present, Absent, or Holiday. You can also use All Present or All Absent to mark everything at once. Hit "Save" when done.

What does the "Holiday" option do?

Marking a subject as "Holiday" (H) means that lecture did not take place. It won't count as an absent or a present and it is excluded from your attendance calculation entirely.

Do I need to mark attendance every day manually?

Yes, attendance needs to be saved each day. However, if your timetable is set up correctly, only the subjects scheduled for that day will appear, so marking is quick. However, even if you miss a day, you can always go back and update your attendance later.

Edit

How do I correct a past attendance entry?

Open the Edit page, select the subject and the date you want to fix, change the status (P / A / H), and click "Update". The change is applied immediately and your stats will reflect the correction.

What do the P, A, and H values show on the Edit page?

These show the current cumulative counts of Present, Absent, and Holiday entries for the selected subject, giving you context before you make any changes.

Stats

What does the Stats page show?

The Stats page displays a table with each subject, the number of classes held, and your current attendance status. Subjects at risk i.e. below 75% are highlighted so you can act fast.

How is the attendance percentage calculated?

It is calculated as: (Classes Present ÷ Total Classes Held) × 100. Holiday entries are not counted in either the numerator or denominator.

Timetable

How do I set up my timetable?

Go to the Timetable page. You will see slots for Monday through Friday. Enter your subjects for each day and click "Save Timetable". The Attendance page will then automatically show only the subjects scheduled for the current day.

Does the timetable support weekends?

Currently the timetable covers Monday to Friday only, which matches the standard BTech college schedule. However, you can easily clone the repository and add weekend support if needed.

Can I update my timetable mid-semester?

Yes. Just open the Timetable page, make your changes, and save again. The updated schedule will apply from the next day onwards. Past records are not affected.

Bunk

How do I use the Bunk Calculator?

On the Bunk page, select a subject, enter the number of upcoming lectures, and set your target percentage. Click "Calculate" to see how many of those upcoming lectures you can safely skip.

What is the "Target Percentage" field?

This is the minimum attendance percentage you want to maintain.

What if I'm already below my target percentage?

If you are already below the target, the calculator will tell you how many upcoming lectures you must attend consecutively to recover and cross back above the target percentage.

Settings

What does the Settings page do?

The Settings page lets you back up and restore your attendance data. You can Export all your data to keep it safe, and Import a previously saved file to restore your records on a new device or after clearing your browser.

How do I export my attendance data?

Open Settings and click the "Export" button. Your attendance data will be copied to your clipboard. Keep this safe as it contains all your subjects, records, and timetable.

How do I import my attendance data?

On the Settings page, click "Import" and Paste the previously exported data from your clipboard. Your data will be restored exactly as it was when you exported it.

Will importing overwrite my current data?

Yes. Importing a file will replace your current attendance data with the data from the imported file. However, you can click "Restore Previous Data" to restore previous data.
