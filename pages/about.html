Why We Built This

Our motivation for developing this project came from a common problem faced by many students. Our college portal does not display attendance percentages, which makes it difficult for students to monitor their attendance status independently. As a result, students often have to rely on faculty members to know their attendance percentage, and there are no alerts or notifications when attendance falls below the required threshold. After discussing this issue with our classmates and seniors, we found that many of them had faced the same challenge. The only available options were either to maintain attendance records manually through personal Excel sheets or repeatedly approach teachers for updates. To solve this problem in a simple and efficient way, we came up with this mini project idea.
